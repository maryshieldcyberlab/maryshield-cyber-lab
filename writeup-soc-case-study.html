Executive Summary

This write-up documents an end-to-end SOC Analyst investigation conducted within the MaryShield Cyber Lab. The exercise demonstrates alert triage, SIEM analysis, endpoint investigation, network analysis, firewall review, evidence correlation, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, containment, recovery, and final reporting.

Incident Scenario

A controlled multi-stage security scenario generated realistic telemetry across multiple defensive platforms. The SOC investigation focused on determining what occurred, which systems were involved, the sequence of activity, and the appropriate response.

Findings

The exercise demonstrated that combining endpoint, network, firewall, identity, and SIEM telemetry provides a more complete and reliable understanding of suspicious activity than relying on any single security platform.

Recommendations

  • Maintain centralized logging.
  • Preserve endpoint telemetry.
  • Monitor authentication activity.
  • Maintain network segmentation.
  • Correlate SIEM, XDR, and IDS evidence.
  • Use MITRE ATT&CK consistently in reporting.
  • Continuously tune detections.
  • Maintain incident-response playbooks.

Lessons Learned

Effective SOC investigations depend on disciplined triage, evidence preservation, timestamp correlation, analyst judgment, and clear documentation.
